READY TO ORDER

Check before ordering, or when the boards arrive (docs/pcb_plan.md):
  - stock: JLCPCB had no CS43131-CNZR (C965907) on 2026-10-31 - pre-order 4 into your JLCPCB parts inventory before the order; LCSC had only 40 of the 10 uH inductor (C354566)
  - touch ribbon: with the display facing up, its gold contacts must face up as well (like the e-paper ribbon's), so they face the bottom-contact connector once folded under the panel
  - plug detect: the 3.5 mm jack's switch opens when a plug goes in, the 4.4 mm jack's is unknown - the firmware sets HPDETECT_INV for each after a try
  - charge timer (owner's decision, docs/pcb_plan.md, Power tree): R3 = 68 k stops a charge after 6.8 to 11.3 h depending on the chip, and the 5000 mAh cell needs about 7.5 to 8.5 h at 0.8 A - leave it (re-plug if the LED blinks), charge at about 1 A, or tie TMR to ground
  - jacks: lay the Kinghelm 4.4 mm jack and a PJ-342 on a 1:1 print of the board before ordering if you can - their footprints come from the drawings, not from parts in hand

Order settings (jlcpcb.com):
  - 6 layers, 1.2 mm thick, 60.8 x 111.8 mm (outline in the Gerbers); layer order top, ground, signal, signal, ground, bottom (the Gerbers' file names: F_Cu, In1_Cu ... In4_Cu, B_Cu)
  - surface finish: ENIG (flat pads for the 0.4 mm-pitch QFNs)
  - stackup JLC06121H-3313 (free; 1.14 mm finished): the USB pairs, 0.2 mm tracks 0.2 mm apart on the top and bottom layers, are about 90 ohm differential over the ground planes 0.0994 mm away (layers 2 and 5); no impedance tolerance needed
  - min track / space 0.1 / 0.1 mm; vias 0.5 / 0.3 mm; no hole under 0.3 mm (smaller ones cost extra)
  - plated slots 0.55 mm wide for the 4.4 mm jack, and one cut-out through the board under it
  - vias filled and capped (free on 6 layers; the chips' centre pads have thermal vias)
  - quantity 5 boards, 2 assembled, top side only. 嘉立创 puts the ESP32 module on its standard line, which takes boards of 70 x 70 mm or more: 5 mm rails on the long edges, held by mouse-bite tabs, not V-cut (the ESP32 is flush with the left edge, the side keys 0.3 mm from both long edges)
